# Track C Summary — Break Phase Structure

## Convergence Verdict

**Hybrid, with continuous prior and discrete confirmation — but both required, neither optional, and continuous is constitutive while discrete is derivative.**

All four domains converge without coordination: none endorses gate-alone (option a); none endorses continuous-alone (option b); all four independently specify that the continuous mode is epistemically prior and the discrete gate is a downstream confirmation that is only interpretable against the continuous record. The four domains are structurally unanimous that choosing between gate and continuous is the wrong question — and that choosing gate-alone produces a specific, named failure mode in each source domain.

The Track C convergence is unusually clean for a distant-domain flux-drive. Four unrelated fields — glass physics, geophysics, Islamic pedagogy, mechanical horology — reach the same architectural conclusion via different mechanisms.

## Deepest Structural Isomorphism

**The isochronism problem** — named by fd-escapement-beat-rate but independently discoverable from the other three domains — is the most non-obvious mechanism that maps.

Isochronism in a balance wheel is the property of maintaining the same rate across all operating amplitudes. A non-isochronous chronometer is not detectable by a trial conducted at one amplitude; it passes certification and fails in service when amplitude changes (temperature excursion, positional variation, spring fatigue).

The isomorphism to Sylveste's Break phase: self-observation health is an isochronism property. A pillar that surfaces contradictions reliably only during high-load sprints (or only under gate-pressure, or only during abnormal operating conditions) is not exhibiting self-observation as a stable faculty. The ≥N gate does not test across operating amplitudes. It can be satisfied entirely during one amplitude — say, a single active-development burst — and the non-isochronous self-observation faculty will not be detected until Epoch, when the amplitude changes.

This mechanism appears in all four domains under different names:
- Lehr agent: the piece that passes polariscope under uniform cooling but cracks under thermal differential (non-uniform stress response)
- Geyser agent: the conduit whose eruption rate is heteroscedastic — the Steamboat pattern that cannot be characterized by a universal window or count
- Sufi agent: the hal that appears under the conditions of the retreat but does not constitute maqam, which must persist in the kitchen as well as the dhikr circle
- Chronometer agent: the non-isochronous balance that holds rate at one amplitude (laboratory) and fails at another (service)

**The unified mechanism: any gate that certifies a property under controlled conditions, without testing that property across operating amplitudes, certifies a thing the gate did not actually measure.** For Break, this means: ≥N receipts accumulated under one operating regime is not evidence that the self-observation faculty is isochronous. The fix requires Break receipts to be drawn from multiple operating regimes (amplitude-variation test), not just accumulated in a window.

## Surprises — Findings Not Anticipated from These Domains

**1. The scoring-authority companionship requirement (fd-suhba-ahwal-discernment)**

The Sufi domain contributed a finding with no software-engineering equivalent: the claim that the *scorer* must have earned standing through prior companionship. In the Naqshbandi tradition, a shaykh cannot perform bay'a for a disciple they have not sat with through ordinary life. The scorer's authority is not a function of method (as software-engineering independence usually is) but of *continuity of presence*.

Applied to Interspect: it is not enough that Interspect is independent of the pillar being scored. Interspect must have sufficient longitudinal observational history with the specific pillar to distinguish that pillar's hal from its maqam. An Interspect with thin observational history is epistemically unqualified to score Break receipts even if its method is sound. This requirement — minimum suhba-window as a precondition for scoring authority — would not appear in any software-audit framework but is structurally precise in the pedagogical tradition.

**2. The dormancy-vs-degradation problem is a P0 finding in the geyser domain**

The vision text (line 464-465) asserts: "a subsystem that cannot find contradictions to surface is a subsystem whose self-observation has gone blind." This is stated as a definitional equivalence. The geyser domain identifies this as a P0 error: dormancy and degradation are not equivalent, and treating silence as definitionally degraded will systematically misflag pillars in steady-state operating conditions. The continuous reservoir record is the only instrument that distinguishes the two. The gate cannot.

This is a direct challenge to a sentence in the current vision text, not a recommendation for a missing feature.

**3. The burst-at-boundary failure mode is independently named by all four agents**

Every agent, from a different causal direction, named the same concrete failure: a pillar accumulates all N receipts near the Compound window boundary and clears the gate. This is:
- Soak-time violation (annealing)
- Burst-eruption without continuous reservoir record (geyser)
- Bay'a-moment burst without suhba (Sufi)
- Kew trial without prior running journal (chronometer)

The unanimity suggests this is not a theoretical failure mode — it is the most predictable gaming surface in the current spec. Any implementer who understands the ≥N gate will see it. The spec does not currently address it.

**4. Tarbiya as a missing lifecycle primitive (fd-suhba-ahwal-discernment)**

The Sufi domain named the absence of a *development pathway* for the self-observation faculty. The spec specifies how to test self-observation (Break receipts) and what to do when it fails (gate-block), but not how to cultivate it. A pillar with weak self-observation has no recovery path other than repeated gate-failure. The tarbiya concept suggests a third option: structured interventions (adversarial FluxBench sessions, paired contrarian agents) that develop the faculty rather than only certifying its presence. This developmental framing has no equivalent in the existing lifecycle vocabulary.

## Implications for §7.1 Revision

Track C's structural convergence points toward three revisions, in priority order:

1. **Isochronism test**: require Break receipts to be drawn from multiple operating amplitudes (high-load and low-load sprint cycles), not just accumulated in a Compound window. This is the single highest-value addition, named by all four domains via different mechanisms.

2. **Distribution requirement on the ≥N threshold**: add a minimum-M-distinct-sprint-cycles requirement to prevent burst-at-boundary. Count alone does not encode the continuous prior. This is a low-cost spec addition with high failure-mode coverage.

3. **Scorer standing requirement**: specify a minimum Interspect observational history requirement (suhba-window) as a precondition for its Break-receipt scores to carry full evidential weight. This is the most novel finding from Track C and has no equivalent in any adjacent review track.
